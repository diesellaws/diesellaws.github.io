Going the extra for the All Star Mile

Racing Victoria required a complete uplift of their All Star Mile website and voting system; a platform that allows the public to help shape the race’s starting lineup. I led the collaborative effort with stakeholders to design and prototype a solution with a strong design, secure voting system, and simplified user experience.

Client

Racing Victoria

Timeline

3 months

Role

Client Liaison, Workshop Facilitation, UI Design, UX Design

Challenge

Design the new All Star Mile website and voting platform with Racing Victoria's refreshed marketing guidelines, ensuring an engaging design, secure voting system, and simplified user experience.

Kickoff Workshop

Our kickoff workshop with Racing Victoria was about figuring out the key drivers for their All-Star Mile website. We focused on getting an overview on the core features during the session, including briefly covering the flow of how users would arrive at the voting page:

  • Feature Prioritisation:

    We ran a quick "weighting" exercise where stakeholders voted on the most important elements for the platform. This helped us prioritise features that would have the biggest impact on the user experience, and allowed us to streamline the interface to only focus on core elements.

  • Page Flow Mapping:

    After various dicussions, we moved to a quick "flow mapping". The main goal here was to confirm how users would move through the platform, from the voting pages to information and advertising sections. We referred to previous page flows and used the whiteboarding session to confirm subtle changes.

Design Process

Given that the platform’s overall structure was largely based on previous years and the page count was minimal, I opted to create and work from high-fidelity UI designs from the start. This approach also allowed us to incorporate Racing Victoria’s brand colors and styles right away, which made it easier for the marketing team to review and provide feedback early on.

Working in high fidelity helped in a few key ways:

  • Quicker Feedback Cycles

    The business team could see a nearly finished version from the beginning, making it easier to visualise the end product and provide relevant feedback.

  • Brand Alignment

    With colors, fonts, and design elements already in place, the marketing team could see how the platform would represent the brand, often minimising back-and-forth revisions.

  • Efficiency in Iterations

    While high-fidelity designs can sometimes slow down early creative changes, in this case, it streamlined the process. By building a lot of the core components earlier on, I was able to make adjustments to the design files rapidly, and start handover with engineers sooner.

The final design maintained a user-friendly flow, similar to past years, but with one key change. Previously, users would click on a horse from the list to view detailed information on a separate page. We streamlined this process by creating a more integrated, single-page experience. Now, the voting list appears on the left with basic details, while the right-hand side displays the full horse profiles. This layout allowed users to quickly browse, review, and vote without unnecessary navigation. This adjustment made the process far more efficient and engaging, contributing to the platform’s overall success.

Core Screens

Each of the platform’s core screens was carefully designed to support a seamless user experience and keep the voting process quick and efficient. By focusing on clarity and functionality, we ensured that each page guided the user smoothly through the necessary steps without unnecessary complexity.

1. Horse Listing and Voting

This page was the starting point for users, showcasing a streamlined list of horses on the left side. Key information such as horse names, rank, and votes was displayed, while detailed profiles for each horse were shown on the right side. This combined design allowed users to make quick comparisons and vote without ever leaving the page, significantly reducing friction in the process.

2. Voting Details

After selecting a horse, users were taken to a simple screen to confirm their choice and enter the required voting details.

3. Mobile Verification

To ensure the integrity of the voting system, this screen asked users to verify their mobile number. We kept this step straightforward and secure, with clear instructions and minimal input fields to make verification as quick and painless as possible.

4. Thank You and Next Steps

The final page thanked users for their vote and outlined the next steps, including what to expect after voting. The design was kept minimal, focusing on clarity while reinforcing Racing Victoria’s branding. This final touch helped drive further engagement including sharing their vote, buying tickets to the track and siging up with Racing+ to watch the event.

Mobile Focus

Every page was designed with a mobile layout built-in, knowing that a fair majority of votes would come from mobile devices. The layout, interactions, and content were optimised for smaller screens, ensuring that users could easily navigate, vote, and verify their information without hassle.

Horse Status and Marketing Guides

On the horse voting details page, there is an interactive status bar located beneath each horse's image, providing real-time updates to the placement of the horse in the race, and the status of the race itself. This feature was essential in communicating each horse's status efficiently while keeping context to the race as a whole.

Additionally, there is a digital clock widget that appeared throughout the site, displaying key countdowns like time until voting opens, leaderboard blackout, voting closes, final field announcement, and All-Star Mile raceday.

To make sure Marketing had a clear guide to work from, we presented the general sizing for all horse images, including the custom star image mask above each horse's picture. These design elements adhered to the marketing team's guidelines, ensuring brand consistency across the platform.

Feedback and Iteration

Racing Victoria gave great feedback on the initial designs, especially the mobile layouts, which resonated well with their expectations. Only minor adjustments were needed, such as tweaking the wording on the mobile confirmation screen for better clarity. We also fine-tuned the flow of information on the voting screen to ensure the best possible user experience.

Overall, working in high fidelity proved to be a smart approach in this case. It allowed us to align much of the content quickly and make necessary refinements with ease, delivering a polished product within the short timeframe.

Delivery and Results

Throughout the project, I worked closely with our engineering team and Project Manager, ensuring smooth collaboration between the front-end design and the back-end development. This close-knit approach allowed us to build the platform efficiently, with the back-end being developed alongside the front-end UI designs. When it came time to hand off the final designs, I shifted to working directly with the engineering team to assist and guide as requested. Racing Victoria was kept in the loop throughout this process, ensuring they were up to date with every key milestone.

Final Results

A key factor in this whole build was the mobile confirmation step, which greatly reduced the number of unverified votes in the system, leading to a much cleaner and more accurate voting process. This also extended to the blocked voting in other countries, blocking unauthorised votes throughout the process. This improvement, along with the more user-friendly interface, resulted in an increase in total votes compared to the previous year and significantly fewer support tickets and “lost” votes.

The project was delivered on time and delivered on Racing Victoria’s expectations. They were a driven and motivated team to work with and I thoroughly enjoyed the process. As this was our first collaboration, it opened the door for future projects.

A new chapter for Off The Track

I led the design and development of Racing Victoria’s Off The Track Community portal, creating a central platform for horse owners, buyers, and fans. This project brought together essential resources for horse welfare, ownership, and education, helping streamline previously manual processes and promote responsible racehorse care post-retirement.

Client

Racing Victoria

Timeline

1 year

Role

UI Design, UX Design

Challenge

Develop a comprehensive platform for Racing Victoria that centralises their programs, simplifies horse ownership processes, and provides resources for horse welfare, while addressing the needs of multiple user groups including owners, trainers, and fans.

Project Kickoff

Racing Victoria’s Off The Track Community portal was designed to revolutionise how the entire lifecycle of racehorses is tracked and managed. The goal was to create a comprehensive platform that supports owners, trainers, and fans in accessing vital information, from racing careers to post-racing pathways and beyond.

This portal would serve as a hub for all things related to horse management, offering users a seamless experience to:

  • Track Horse Lifecycle:

    Monitor the journey of horses from their racing career through retirement and rehoming.

  • Ownership Transfers:

    Simplify the process of claiming and transferring ownership of horses at any stage.

  • Post-Racing Opportunities:

    Provide insights into career transitions for horses, including equestrian sports, therapy, and companionship roles.

  • Emergency Contacts:

    Allow horse owners to register emergency contacts for immediate support and response.

  • Educational Content:

    Offer training, care guides, and resources for managing horses at various stages of their lives.

  • Community Events:

    Connect users with exclusive events and opportunities to engage with the Off The Track community.

By integrating all aspects of horse management, this portal would simplify processes, promote responsible ownership, and foster a more engaged, informed community.

Research and Design Process

Given the extensive scope of the Off The Track Community portal, the project required several months of in-depth research and collaboration. We began by thoroughly understanding Racing Victoria’s goals and the needs of their diverse user base, from horse owners to trainers and fans.

Our team conducted comprehensive brainstorming and information-gathering sessions to map out the essential programs that would form the core of the portal. These programs included:

  • Acknowledged Retrainers Program:

    Connecting horses with certified retrainers for new career opportunities.

  • RESET Program:

    Evaluation, support, education, and transition programs for racehorses.

  • Full Circle Program:

    Tracking the long-term well-being and status of previously owned horses.

  • Off The Track Events:

    Organizing community events for owners, trainers, and fans to connect.

  • OHEP:

    A program for providing humane end-of-life care for horses in need.

  • Claiming and Thoroughbred Profiles:

    A system to manage horse profiles and facilitate ownership claims.

Once the structure and priorities were established, we moved into the design phase. This involved creating a series of wireframes that laid out the architecture and user flow of the portal. The initial wireframes served as a foundation for discussions and iterations with Racing Victoria, ensuring alignment at each stage of development.

The scale of the project required multiple rounds of revisions and refinements, transitioning from low-fidelity wireframes to fully interactive, high-fidelity designs. Each wireframe and high-fidelity design was built with flexibility in mind, to account for future updates and program expansions.

From Wireframes to High-Fidelity

The design process began with creating wireframes to establish the foundational architecture and user flow of the Off The Track Community portal. These wireframes served as the blueprint for discussion and feedback sessions with Racing Victoria. Through several iterations, we refined the structure and interaction flows until we had a solid base to move forward.

After aligning on the wireframes, the next step was to develop high-fidelity designs that reflected the final user experience. We built reusable design components in Sketch/Figma, allowing for consistency and efficiency when transitioning to development handover.

The high-fidelity designs were created with flexibility in mind, featuring modular elements that could easily adapt to future updates or new requirements. Key areas, such as the Claim Thoroughbred flow, evolved from simple wireframes into interactive, polished screens that maintained clarity and ease of use.

Simplifying Complex Forms

One of the major improvements was in simplifying forms and data entry. Previously managed via paper and email, the new portal digitised these processes, significantly reducing manual work for the Off The Track team. We designed forms to be flexible, allowing sections to be added or removed with ease while maintaining a user-friendly interface with clear labels, buttons, and guidance notes.

Search and Claim Features

Another major feature was the Search and Claim flow for thoroughbred profiles. We designed a robust search system that could handle both 'unclaimed' and 'claimed' thoroughbreds, allowing users to quickly filter by tags like age, color, and status. This made the process of finding available horses or managing claimed horses simple and efficient.

Admin Dashboard

One of the key requirements was the creation of a dedicated admin dashboard. This feature transformed the way the team at Racing Victoria managed forms and submissions, significantly reducing the time spent on manual processes.

Previously, forms were submitted via paper or email, requiring manual uploads into a database; an inefficient process that consumed hours and hindered scalability. The new admin dashboard streamlined this by allowing admins to review, edit, and approve or deny forms directly within the system. With forms automatically sorted into sections, team members could easily track submissions, assign tasks, and provide timely feedback. Digital submission of Thoroughbred profiles enabled instant review, with admins able to request information or approve forms in just a few clicks, significantly speeding up workflows and improving the user experience while saving countless hours of manual work.

By reducing the friction between form submission and review, the portal enabled greater efficiency, allowing the team to focus additional attention on meaningful work related to horse welfare and program development.

Final Launch and Impact

After a year of rigorous design, development, and testing, Racing Victoria launched the inaugural version of the Off The Track Community portal to a select group of Acknowledged Retrainers. The feedback from this initial group was invaluable, particularly highlighting areas where complex form submissions required further streamlining. This insight allowed us to refine the platform ahead of its broader release.

The full launch to owners, retrainers, and staff marked a significant milestone for Racing Victoria. The portal's modular design ensured it could easily adapt to future changes, making it simple for the team to modify sections and layouts as needed. Within weeks of going live, the simplified form submissions and user-friendly interface delivered noticeable improvements in operational efficiency—saving countless hours previously spent on manual data entry.

Beyond operational benefits, the platform fostered stronger connections within the Off The Track community. Racing Victoria was able to provide better support, guidance, and education to users, while also showcasing the benefits of each program. This new level of engagement allowed the team to fulfill their mission more effectively, enhancing the overall experience for everyone involved and cementing the portal as a crucial tool for the future of horse lifecycle management.

CrownBet's core expansion

Over the course of a year, I played a pivotal role in improving CrownBet’s app experience, integrating William Hill's user base following a major acquisition, and delivering features to enhance user engagement. The focus was on creating a seamless experience in a highly regulated industry while guiding transitions during the rebrand to BetEasy.

Client

CrownBet

Timeline

1 year

Role

UI Design, UX Design, App Development, Workshop Facilitation

Challenge

Improve the core app experience for CrownBet users while ensuring seamless migration of William Hill customers post-acquisition. Additionally, lead the effort to uplift the app's UI/UX in a heavily regulated industry.

Project Kickoff

This project spanned several key areas; uplifting CrownBet’s core website/app experience and integrating William Hill's customer base. The highly regulated nature of betting apps added extra layers of complexity in terms of approvals and discussions, ensuring every feature and UI change was carefully considered.

My role involved deep collaboration across teams, including engineering, compliance, marketing, and product management, to deliver a modernised, user-friendly experience for a rapidly growing customer base.

Improving the BetSlip and Reward Features

One of the core app areas I helped uplift early on was the BetSlip, a crucial part of the user experience. I added features that improved the display of bonus bets and highlighted the rewards store, which drove engagement and usage of these features. A lot of these features needed to be added before the migration to ensure continuity for the William Hill customers.

This enhancement (yellow highlights) directly contributed to better user engagement, offering an intuitive interface where users could easily access their bonuses and rewards.

  • Core Markets Tabs:

    The app’s layout was adjusted by adding tabbed sections directly on the home page to allow viewing all the additional markets that were being introduced as part of the acquisition.

  • Bet Boost Inline Notifications:

    Various betting features such as Bet Boost were going unnoticed, and with minimal education. We enhanced the Betslip to call out remaining Bet Boosts while also providing a way to read more about the benefits. This led to increased usage of Bet Boosts across the entire platform.

  • Rewards Store Highlighting:

    The Rewards Store was previously buried and as such Bonus Bets (free bets for customers) were not being used. By making this section more obvious, and providing more education, it allowed for stronger engagement around this area.

Collaborative Workshops for Feature Ideation

Throughout the project, I led high-energy workshops aimed at generating innovative app features to boost user engagement. By adapting Google’s Design Sprint methodology into 3-hour Feature Sprints (FeDS), we transformed brainstorming into a focused, fast-paced, and fun process. These sessions sparked key ideas that later enhanced the app, such as predictors, betting comparisons, and personalised segments for users based on their experience.

Workshop Highlights:

  • Superpowers Icebreaker:

    Kick-started creativity and broke the ice with a fun mini-game where participants shared their unique "superpowers".

  • Funky 4’s:

    A playful twist on "Crazy 8’s" where each participant sketched four creative solutions to real-world challenges, ranging from practical to wild.

  • Vote and Share:

    Teams voted on the most promising ideas, ensuring decisions weren't swayed by popularity but by value and innovation.

  • Team Collaboration:

    Participants split into teams to develop feature solutions, constantly validating against business and customer needs.

Workshop Results:

These workshops not only energised the team but directly led to actionable features that were quickly transformed into wireframes and successfully implemented in the app. This collaborative, fast-paced approach encouraged creativity while ensuring efficiency.

William Hill Acquisition and Customer Migration

During the $300M acquisition of William Hill, our team assisted new customers in transitioning smoothly onto the CrownBet platform, with app and onboarding feaures aligned to their existing experience. This involved the migration of their data, account details, and providing clear communication on how features were renamed or modified within the new app. We also collaborated with our Sydney agency during this transition, to make sure all our wording and branding was aligned.

Homepage Uplift and Rebranding to BetEasy

I was also a key player in the effort to modernise CrownBet’s homepage, providing a fresh, uplifting experience that aligned with the transition to BetEasy. This involved incorporating new branding elements and ensuring consistency across the platform to reflect the rebranded identity.

My work helped ensure a smooth transition during the rebranding phase, maintaining user trust while introducing a new visual identity and experience.

Overall Results

The merger between CrownBet and William Hill was executed smoothly, with nearly all William Hill users successfully transitioning to our platform without major issues. The seamless migration of user data, accounts, and preferences ensured minimal disruption, maintaining trust and satisfaction among the newly acquired user base.

Post-merger, we expanded and enhanced the app's core features, particularly around the BetSlip, personalised betting experiences, and user rewards. These improvements increased engagement and usage across both the app and the website.

Our focus then shifted to facilitating the complete rebrand from CrownBet to BetEasy. This transformation involved updating the platform’s design, refining the user interface, and ensuring a consistent brand experience across all touchpoints. The rebrand was highly successful, maintaining user retention while introducing a fresh, modern identity to the market.

Uplifting the MyPost dashboard

Our team were tasked with uplifting the MyPost Dashboard; a platform designed to simplify how customers track, manage, and interact with the MyPost services. Our goal was to create a more intuitive onboarding experience, integrate visual styles from marketing, and make it accessible for all users (from tech-savvy to those less familiar with digital platforms).

Client

Australia Post

Timeline

6 months

Role

UX Research, UI Design, Team Collaboration, Stakeholder Engagement, Workshop Facilitation

Challenge

Uplift the MyPost Dashboard and onboarding experience to create a more user-friendly, visually appealing interface that would be easy to navigate for all types of users, while incorporating AusPost's marketing visual style and ensuring functionality across various devices.

Initial Research and Wireframing

Our project began with a series of kickoff workshops with key stakeholders and internal teams to define the primary goals for the MyPost Dashboard redesign. The initial challenge was to create a flow that was easy to understand, visually cohesive, and guided users through core functions like parcel tracking and service management.

We focused on two primary goals:

  • User Education and Onboarding:

    We needed to design an intuitive way to guide new users through the dashboard, ensuring they understood the platform's core features - such as tracking and managing their parcels, redirecting deliveries, using the apps to manage on the move and setting up their accounts with additional services.

  • Visual and Structural Overhaul:

    We aimed to incorporate the new visual style provided by AusPost's marketing team. This involved stronger color coding, modern card-based designs, and clearer imagery to enhance user engagement and accessibility.

We started by gathering insights from the existing platform, identifying pain points, and mapping out key interactions. During this phase, our team and I captured various elements of the current website; creating wireframes on a whiteboard to help us visualise how to bring these elements together in a cohesive flow.

The wireframes allowed us to take a step back and analyse how to structure the information and functionality in a way that was both user-friendly and in line with the new visual guidelines.

Stacking the Updated Structure

Next, we printed out all the existing parts of the website and stacked them vertically in the flow we had decided upon. This exercise highlighted several key issues: inconsistent design patterns, unclear hierarchies, and scattered explanations. Not to mention that each one of these sections were in many areas across the entire platform - so pulling them all together was crucial.

By seeing all of the current pages and sections laid out together, we gained a clearer understanding of the redundancies and confusion that users were likely facing. This reinforced the need for a thorough overhaul; not just a visual refresh, but a reimagining of the structure and functionality of the dashboard.

Visual Uplift Storytelling

With a solid understanding of the current state, we began the process of redesigning the MyPost Dashboard from the ground up. Over time we designed a refreshed vertical flow that introduced the brand’s updated visual language, using a modern, card-based layout, strong color palettes, and large imagery/icons to guide users through the platform more intuitively. We connected this to the discussed flow presented on the wall to make sure it aligned.

Our goal was not only to uplift the design but also to improve the overall storytelling of the user journey. Each section was carefully restructured to flow logically, with clear entry points for tracking parcels, managing deliveries, and accessing additional services. We also ensured the entire flow adhered to accessibility standards to cater to a wide range of users.

User Testing and Feedback

Understanding the users' needs was key to this uplift. We conducted several UX research and testing sessions, gathering insights through stakeholder interviews, user surveys, and A/B testing. One of our primary goals was to cater to a wide range of users, from those comfortable with technology to those who may struggle with it.

We invited various users of the platform in for some in-person wireframe testing, making sure the simplified flow had enough touchpoints and education that they could understand the service.

Some of the items we confirmed:

  • Introductory Video

    The header featured a video introducing MyPost’s services, which users found to be an engaging and effective way to quickly learn about core functions. Many users, especially those unfamiliar with MyPost, said this video gave them a better insight into the full range of features.

  • Delivery Options – 24/7 Parcel Lockers, Parcel Collect, PO Boxes

    This section highlighted the various ways users could manage deliveries beyond traditional home delivery. Users appreciated the clear breakdown of options and some mentioned they weren’t aware of these options before and felt more confident using them after going through the flow.

  • Delivery Management – Track and Redirect

    The flow emphasised delivery management on the go, allowing users to track parcels in real-time, choose to leave them in a safe place, or redirect them to a new address. Users praised the simplicity of this process, noting how the improved flow made it intuitive to manage deliveries while parcels were en route.

  • Mobile App Integration

    A section prompting users to download the mobile app was clearly laid out, and users responded positively to the suggestion of using the app for easier management of their deliveries. Many users, especially those who preferred managing on the go, said the app would be their preferred method of staying updated on their parcels.

  • Promotional Space – Seasonal Sales and Offers

    The promotional section featured banners for upcoming events like Christmas sales or special deals from merchants. Users understood this space was reserved for marketing, and several commented that they liked having timely offers incorporated into the dashboard without it feeling intrusive.

Internal Uplift

Carrying on the feedback and decisions from the dashboard, we shifted our focus to uplifting the internal sections of the platform. This involved restructuring the layout and prioritising features based on user interest and overall usability. To achieve this, we conducted a collaborative session where the team used a "t-shirt sizing" method to assess and rank features. We also used post-it note weighting exercises to determine which elements should be emphasised based on their importance and user value.

We worked through this exercise to conceptualise the updated internal sections, ensuring that every feature was thoughtfully placed according to its relevance and usage. This allowed us to create a more intuitive and accessible interface for managing internal operations and services.

Internal Demo Day

Once the internal sections were finalised, we organised an internal demo day to present our work to stakeholders, product owners, and other teams within the company. The demo day was a major milestone for the project, as it allowed us to showcase the before-and-after transformations of the MyPost Dashboard, along with the new flows and design updates.

The presentations were printed out and laid across desks in large paper flows, comparing the old designs with the new. We walked stakeholders through each flow, highlighting key improvements in navigation, user education, and visual consistency.

The presentation was very well received, with stakeholders praising the clarity of the new design and how well our work integrated with the overall visual and functional uplift of other internal teams. Our collaboration across teams ensured that the platform felt cohesive and user-friendly, reflecting Australia Post’s brand and service goals.

Over the next few months different pieces of the uplifted designs rolled out to users. Throughout the roll out we had great feedback, and many users praising the simpler explanation of all the service offerings.

Lighting the way for LIFX

Over the course of a year, I led the design effort to uplift the LIFX app, create new packaging for their smart bulbs, and develop marketing materials. The project aimed to refresh the user interface, improve the product’s visibility on store shelves, and provide clear, engaging marketing support.

Client

LIFX

Timeline

1 year

Role

UI Design, UX Design, Packaging Design, Print Marketing

Challenge

Redesign the LIFX app to improve usability, create cohesive packaging for three smart bulb products, and design marketing materials to support sales and customer understanding of the product’s features.

Project Kickoff

The LIFX project involved three core areas: redesigning the app, updating the packaging for their lightbulbs, and creating marketing materials to educate and inspire customers. I collaborated closely with the product, marketing, and engineering teams to ensure consistency and quality across these touchpoints.

Each part of the project had its own unique challenges. The app redesign required modernising an interface that hadn’t changed in years, while keeping existing features familiar to users. The packaging needed to stand out in a crowded market of traditional and smart lighting solutions. Lastly, the marketing materials had to effectively communicate the advantages of LIFX’s smart bulbs, both to customers and sales staff.

App Redesign Process

As the app had remained largely unchanged for over two years, with several features buried deep within the interface, the goal was to bring these features (namely Scenes and Schedules) to the forefront while keeping the app intuitive and easy to use for existing customers.

By opting to work directly with high-fidelity designs, I was able to bring LIFX’s brand colors and style into the process from the very beginning. This approach helped the marketing and product teams visualise the final experience early on, accelerating feedback and ensuring strong alignment from the outset.

  • Collapsible Modules:

    The app’s layout was adjusted by adding expandable areas to the main feed, highlighting Scenes, Schedules, and Integrations. This kept the interface clean and straightforward while giving users quick access to advanced features like automation without digging through menus.

  • Unified Control Screen:

    To simplify user interactions, controls were consolidated into a single-page interface. Users could turn lights on or off, adjust color settings, and manage schedules all from one screen, reducing unnecessary navigation.

  • Connected Widgets:

    The widgets were a very common use case for quick adjustments and settings across a customers home. I brought across the same design elements and styles to align the experience - as in various cases the widgets would be the sole view of LIFX, with some users never needing to open the app again post install.

  • Dark Mode:

    The whole app was reimagined with dark mode in mind - based on some great feedback from users finding the screens too bright when modifying their lights at night. This allowed us to stand out from the pack with an interface that was different from the outset.

With these improvements, the app maintained its core functionality but was significantly more user-friendly and visually appealing, reflecting the modern capabilities of LIFX smart bulbs.

Packaging Redesign

When it came to the packaging, we wanted to break away from the standard approach of product photography on a flat color background. The challenge was to create a design that reflected the innovation behind LIFX’s smart bulbs while also standing out on store shelves.

Design Iterations

I led the process for the packaging of the the White 800 and the Color 1000 smart bulbs - The Color 1000 being the successor to the LIFX Original. I experimented with various design concepts, aiming for clean, modern aesthetics that aligned with the LIFX brand.

The White 800 packaging, for example, focused on simplicity and functionality, using a predominantly white and cream backgrounds with minimal text. For the Color 900 and 1000, the packaging emphasised vibrant colors and bold typography to highlight the bulb’s versatility.

After the development on the White 800 and the Color 1000, I expanded to the BR30 range of White 900 and Color 1000.

Feedback and Refinement

I gathered feedback from both customers, various stores and internal teams throughout the process, leading to several iterations. The final packaging was designed not only to catch the eye but also to clearly communicate the key features of each product.

Sales Marketing Booklet

To support sales staff and enhance customer education, I developed a series of 12+ page marketing booklets that highlighted the key features and benefits of LIFX bulbs. These booklets were designed to be simple, clear, and visually engaging, making them easy to reference during customer interactions.

For the White 800, the booklet focused on the bulb’s Wi-Fi connectivity, energy efficiency, and ease of use. It was important that the content communicated these technical details in a way that was approachable, helping both sales teams and customers understand the value of the product quickly. For most customers, LIFX would be their first experience with interactive LED bulbs, and so the experience had to fresh but familiar.

Feedback and Iteration

Throughout the year, we gathered feedback from various stakeholders, internal teams, customers, and sales partners. The redesigned app was praised for its clean layout and ease of use, particularly the new collapsible sections for advanced features. The packaging, too, was well received for its simplicity and bold, modern look.

Minor tweaks were made to the marketing booklets based on feedback, such as clarifying technical specifications and adjusting design elements for better readability. These refinements ensured that the materials were as effective as possible in communicating the product’s strengths.

Delivery and Results

The app redesign, packaging, and marketing materials were successfully delivered throughout the year, and subsequent year for the rollout of the new packaging. The app saw increased user engagement, particularly in the usage of scenes and automation features, while the new packaging helped differentiate LIFX products in a competitive market.

The marketing booklets provided a helpful tool for sales teams, increasing their confidence in explaining LIFX’s smart lighting capabilities to customers. The project was a success, improving the overall customer experience and boosting brand visibility both in-app and on the shelves.
