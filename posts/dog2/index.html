5 Common UX Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

July 1, 2022

Good user experience comes from clarity, consistency and attention to how people actually move through a product. These are five common problems worth catching early.

1. Designing without a clear user goal

Every screen should help someone complete a meaningful task. Start with the user's intended outcome before deciding on layout, content or interaction patterns.

2. Weak visual hierarchy

When everything has equal visual weight, users struggle to decide what matters. Use scale, spacing, colour and contrast to establish a clear reading order.

3. Inconsistent interaction patterns

Buttons, links and controls should behave predictably. Reuse patterns throughout the experience and avoid creating a new interaction when an established one already works.

4. Ignoring edge cases

Empty states, errors, long labels and slow connections are part of the product. Design these states alongside the ideal flow so the experience remains useful under pressure.

5. Testing too late

Early feedback is cheaper and more useful than feedback after implementation. Test rough flows and wireframes before visual polish makes the work feel too finished to change.
